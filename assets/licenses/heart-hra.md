Retrieved 2026-10-23. Exact asset URLs, pinned SHA-256 hashes, citations and original metadata/crosswalk URLs are in `assets/sources/heart-hra.json`. Acquisition uses public versioned GLBs, not clinical images or private patient records. These are Visible Human-based atlas reconstructions, not this project's direct medical image segmentation.

Changes: selected 14 heart and 30 matching male vasculature source meshes, added semantic identifiers and provenance; glTF → Blender → glTF axis roundtrip. Original coordinates and all nonduplicate oriented triangles are retained. Blender's automatic mesh validation removes exactly **18 duplicate-index faces: RA 12, LM 5, SVC 1**, keeping one original winding per duplicate-index group. The selected source's **267,360 triangles become 267,342 output triangles**, with **zero observed coordinate deviation**. The output still contains **835 zero-area faces: RA 812, LM 6, SVC 17**. This narrowly recorded importer cleanup is not anatomical repair or a claim that all topology is unchanged. The exported source-derived GLB adds no smoothing, remeshing, nonuniform scaling, invented anatomy, textures or motion. The browser applies educational cardiac deformation, reconstructed valve leaflets and flow guides; these are CardioLab additions, not measured source motion. Browser colors are illustrative material presets. Source label conflicts and remaining mesh defects require review. Source reviewers listed upstream are **not** project reviewers and do not approve this derivative.
